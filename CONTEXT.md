# Domain glossary

Terms with a fixed meaning in this project. Definitions here are about
the domain, never about implementation.

## Documentation

- **Consumer documentation** — the guides and reference pages
  (`docs/guides/`, `docs/reference/`). Written for a developer *using*
  pascal-mcp-sdk in their own program, who never needs the contributor
  toolchain. This is the surface held to the gold standard.
- **Contributor documentation** — the internals pages
  (`docs/internals/`) plus the root governance files. Written for a
  person changing the library itself, through the lens of "I want to
  contribute a feature", "how do I fix a bug", "what are the
  workflows". The consumer and contributor lenses are distinct and
  must never be collapsed into one document.
- **Terminal recording (cast)** — a captured session of the real,
  built binaries running in a real terminal. Staged, hand-synthesized,
  or mocked-up animations are not terminal recordings and do not
  appear in the documentation.
